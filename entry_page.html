Local Organization Accreditation Portal

Official Registry and Management System

Welcome to the official accreditation system. This platform is designed to register, validate, and monitor local organizations, ensuring they meet the necessary compliance standards for community operation.

Why is Accreditation Important?

  • Official Recognition: Legitimizes your organization within the local government and community structures (e.g., Barangay-based or Regional).
  • Access to Funding & Support: Opens doors for local grants, fund-raising opportunities, and official partnerships with registering agencies like DSWD or DOLE.
  • Community Impact: Allows active participation in local body priorities, such as health councils, disaster risk reduction, and solid waste management.

By proceeding to the management portal, authorized personnel can perform CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) operations to register new organizations, update existing records, manage membership counts, and review accreditation renewals.
